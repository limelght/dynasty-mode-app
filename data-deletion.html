Request Data Deletion by Email

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email sent64781914@gmail.com from the address tied to your DynastyHQ account.
  2. Include your display name and, if relevant, the league names you want us to review.
  3. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - DynastyHQ.

What We Delete

  • Account profile information associated with your DynastyHQ user record.
  • Saved notification destinations and linked delivery identifiers tied to your account.
  • League membership records that are solely associated with your account, where operationally feasible.

What We May Retain

We may retain limited operational, security, fraud-prevention, audit, or legal compliance data where necessary. In shared leagues, some league history may remain if required to preserve league operations or administrative records for other users.

Response Timing

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within a reasonable time and process verified requests as promptly as practical.
