DynastyHQ

Privacy Policy

Effective June 9, 2026

DynastyHQ helps commissioners and league members manage college football dynasty leagues, share updates, and receive notifications across email, browser push, Telegram, Discord, and WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can request deletion.

Open App Terms of Service Data Deletion

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your email address, display name, and authentication identifiers.
  • League information such as league names, invite codes, membership roles, notification preferences, and commissioner updates.
  • Optional contact and delivery information including webhook URLs, Telegram chat IDs, WhatsApp numbers, and browser push subscriptions.
  • Operational metadata needed to secure the service, including backoffice verification sessions and notification job processing logs.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • To create, operate, archive, reinstate, and manage dynasty leagues.
  • To send commissioner updates and member notifications through channels users enable.
  • To protect the platform, verify administrator access, and troubleshoot delivery issues.

Third-Party Services

DynastyHQ uses third-party infrastructure and communication providers including Firebase, Resend or other SMTP email services, Telegram, Discord, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, and browser push services. These providers process data only to deliver the features you request.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to operate DynastyHQ, send notifications, secure the platform, and comply with applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain account and league data for as long as the account remains active or as needed to operate DynastyHQ, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and maintain platform security. Notification delivery artifacts and administrative audit data may be retained for operational and abuse-prevention reasons.

Your Choices

  • You may update notification destinations and preferences from within the app.
  • You may request account and associated data deletion using the process described on the Data Deletion page.
  • You may disable browser notifications at the device or browser level.
